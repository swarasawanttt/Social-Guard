AI-Based Analysis of Fake and Bot Social Accounts

A machine-learning tool that estimates whether an Instagram, Twitter/X or Facebook account looks Real, Fake or Bot, based on its public profile statistics. Built as an academic project — results are decision support, not proof.

How It Works

The model looks at public profile statistics such as followers, following, posts, bio and username patterns. It does not read post content or the follower network.

public

Three Platforms

One model trained on Instagram, Twitter/X and Facebook data together. Its reliability differs by platform, and other platforms are not supported.

bolt

Simple to Use

Enter a profile link or username to get a result. If a profile can’t be read automatically, you can type its stats in by hand or upload screenshots.

precision_manufacturing

Confidence Scores

Every result comes with a confidence score. When the model isn’t confident enough, it says Inconclusive instead of forcing a label — and it can still be wrong.

fingerprint
policy

Explained Results

Each result lists the main reasons behind it (using SHAP and LIME) and can be downloaded as a PDF report that includes the model’s known limitations.

Good to Know

The Social Guard is a proof-of-concept trained on public research datasets. Its Real, Fake and Bot categories follow those datasets’ definitions, sophisticated bots can slip through, and very large or verified accounts may get an Inconclusive result. Never use a result as the only basis for a decision about a person.
